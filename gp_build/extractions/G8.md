# G8 — Water-ENTRY impact / water-EXIT / trans-medium configuration

Theme (G8): water-entry IMPACT force & peak DECELERATION (target monomial
`a_peak = C·V^a·d^b·m^c`, hoped exponents 2, 2, −1), cavity formation/pinch-off,
entry-angle & speed effects, added-mass-during-entry, water-EXIT lift/thrust,
and Ma et al.'s HAUV configuration relations.

> **KEY RESULT up front:** none of the four assigned sources prints `a_peak = C·V^a·d^b·m^c`
> as a fitted law, **but song2020 supplies the physical justification for exactly those
> exponents**: it defines the impact/drag force as `F = c_D · ½ρV² · (π/4)D²` with c_D
> **nose-shape-dominated and nearly independent of V, mass and (post-cavity) entry angle**.
> Dividing by mass gives `a_peak = F/m = c_D·(π/8)·ρ_w·V²·D²/m` → **a=2, b=2, c=−1**,
> matching the the project design notes pipeline target. song2020's finding "c_D stable once the supercavity
> forms, contributions of mass/speed/angle secondary" is precisely what makes that a clean
> **monomial to fit from interFoam** (C ← nose/cavitator shape). This is the load-bearing find.

LaTeX: $$C_{L}=\frac{L_{r}}{\rho_{air}\,\omega_{r}^{2}\,D_{r}^{4}}\qquad\Rightarrow\qquad L_r = C_L\,\rho_{air}\,\omega_r^{2}\,D_r^{4}\tag{1}$$

### E-9 ★ Impact/drag force & peak-deceleration monomial (KEY)  [block: entry]
LaTeX:
$$c_{D}=\frac{F}{\tfrac12\rho V^{2}A},\quad A=\tfrac14\pi D^{2}\ \Rightarrow\
F=c_{D}\,\tfrac12\rho V^{2}\,\tfrac{\pi}{4}D^{2}\ \Rightarrow\
\boxed{\,a_{peak}=\frac{F}{m}=c_{D}\,\frac{\pi}{8}\,\frac{\rho_w\,V^{2}\,D^{2}}{m}\,}$$
→ monomial exponents $V^{2}$, $D^{2}$, $m^{-1}$ i.e. **(a,b,c) = (2, 2, −1)**.
Context: **THE water-entry peak-deceleration law the GP needs.** F is the slamming/impact axial force;
its peak occurs the instant the nose contacts water, then decays as the supercavity forms.
Validity / why the exponents are clean: song2020's central numerical finding is that the **stable c_D
is dominated by wetted-nose shape; "the contributions of mass and the entry speed are less significant"**
and "effects of entry angle are less significant except the initial stage" — so c_D ≈ const(nose) and
the V², D², m⁻¹ scaling is genuinely monomial. C (the lumped coefficient c_D·π/8) is the single
number to calibrate.
GP-class: **monomial** | Handling: **fit→posynomial coefficient from CFD (interFoam) — THE key a_peak fit** | Loc: §4, Fig.20 & Fig.23, p.TBD
Notes: This is the empirical backbone for pipeline step [5a] `a_peak = C·V^a·d^b·m^c (2,2,−1)` and the
[7] gate `a_peak ≤ 20 g`. song2020 confirms exponents from first principles; interFoam supplies C for
*this* nose/cavitator. Peak (transient, added-mass-driven) c_D is higher than the stable value — fit the
**peak**, not the supercavity-stable, c_D for the g-load constraint.

### E-10 Entry-angle effect on peak impact force (prose + Fig.23)  [block: entry]

GP-class: monomial-augmentable (add $(\sin\gamma)^n$ factor, GP-legal) | Handling: fit→posynomial (extra exponent from interFoam α-sweep) | Loc: §4 Fig.23, p.TBD
Notes: physical basis = added-mass growth rate ($F\sim d(m_a V)/dt$) at initial wetting; peak force is an
**added-mass-during-entry** phenomenon, not steady drag. Keep — it couples entry g to entry angle.

### E-11 Non-dimensional pressure (pressure/cavitation coefficient)  [block: entry]

Notes: nose pressure asymmetry at shallow β causes trajectory deflection ("ricochet/tumble") — the

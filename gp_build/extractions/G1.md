# G1 — GP/SP machinery & data-fitting methods (extraction)

Theme: the tools to BUILD the GP/SP sizing model — monomial/posynomial/signomial
definitions & GP standard form (to cite), posynomial-equality relaxation, the
"minimize the inverse to maximize" trick, the CFD→GP data-fitting methods
(max-affine / softmax-affine / implicit-softmax / gpfit), and reusable canonical
aircraft GP constraint templates (L=W, drag polar, Breguet, wing-box, prop efficiency).

Convention note: several sources use the SAME lift monomial example (½ρV²C_L S), so
definitions are near-identical across Hoburg 2014, Boyd 2007, softmax paper, thesis,
effmdo, gpintro. Nomenclature is quarantined per source below. Equation tags in
`(n)` are the SOURCE's own numbering.

## Geometric Programming for Aircraft Design Optimization — bibkey: hoburg2014geometric — file: 2012_gp_design (1).mmd
Bib: Hoburg, W. & Abbeel, P., "Geometric Programming for Aircraft Design Optimization,"
AIAA Journal, Vol. 52, No. 11, 2014, pp. 2414–2426. doi:10.2514/1.J052732.
(OCR of the 2012 preprint version; content matches published paper.)

Nomenclature (this source):
- **u** = decision-variable vector ∈ ℝⁿ₊₊ ; a = exponent vector ; c = positive coefficient
- h(u) = monomial ; f(u) = posynomial ; C_D, C_L, C_dp = drag/lift/profile-drag coeffs
- k = pressure-drag form factor ; e = Oswald efficiency ; A = aspect ratio ; τ = t/c
- θ_fuel = fuel mass fraction ; η_0 = overall efficiency ; η_i,η_v = inviscid/viscous prop eff
- λ = taper ratio ; p ≡ 1+2λ, q ≡ 1+λ (change of vars) ; ν(λ)=(λ²+λ+1)/(1+λ)²
- Ĩ = I/c⁴ (area moment of inertia per chord⁴) ; S_s section modulus ; M_r root moment
- CLASH: their λ = taper ratio (NOT a Lagrange multiplier; contrast thesis §3 where λ = dual var)

### E-1 Monomial definition  [block: method]
LaTeX: $h(\mathbf{u})=c\prod_{i=1}^{n} u_i^{a_i},\quad c\in\mathbb{R}_{++},\ \mathbf{a}\in\mathbb{R}^{n}$  (eq 2)
Context: canonical monomial def. Example given: lift ½ρV²C_L S is a monomial in (ρ,V,C_L,S) with c=½, a=(1,2,1,1). Exponents may be negative/non-integer. h(u)>0 always.
GP-class: monomial | Handling: use-as-is (cite) | Loc: §II.1, p.TBD
Notes: THE definition to cite for "what is a monomial" in our report.

### E-2 Posynomial definition  [block: method]
LaTeX: $f(\mathbf{u})=\sum_{k=1}^{K} c_k\prod_{i=1}^{n} u_i^{a_{ik}},\quad c_k\in\mathbb{R}_{++}$  (eq 3)
Context: sum of monomials; all monomials are 1-term posynomials. `0.23+u₁²+0.1u₁u₂^-0.1` is a posynomial; `2u₁−u₂^-1` is NOT (negative leading coeff).
GP-class: posynomial | Handling: use-as-is (cite) | Loc: §II.2, p.TBD

### E-3 GP standard form  [block: method]
LaTeX: $\min\ f_0(\mathbf{u})\ \text{s.t.}\ f_i(\mathbf{u})\le1\ (i=1..m),\ h_i(\mathbf{u})=1$  (eq 4)
Context: minimize posynomial objective s.t. posynomial ≤1 and monomial =1. Closed under monomial division ⇒ (posy ≤ mono) and (mono = mono) are GP-compatible. Any monomial equality h=1 ≡ h≤1 ∧ 1/h≤1.
GP-class: — (framework) | Handling: use-as-is (cite) | Loc: §II.3, p.TBD
Notes: Log change of vars → monomials become affine, posynomials become log-sum-exp (convex). This is why GP → global optimum, no initial guess.

### E-4 GP-compatible model forms  [block: method]
LaTeX: (1) $1\ge f(\mathbf{u})$ (f posy/mono); (2) $1=h(\mathbf{u})$ (h mono); and by monomial division $h(\mathbf{u})\ge f(\mathbf{u})$, $h_1(\mathbf{u})=h_2(\mathbf{u})$.
Context: the "allowed shapes" checklist we test every candidate constraint against.
GP-class: — | Handling: use-as-is (cite) | Loc: §V intro, p.TBD

### E-5 Posynomial equality RELAXATION (the central technique)  [block: method]
LaTeX: equality $C_D=C_{d_0}+\dfrac{C_L^2}{\pi e A}$ (eq 13, non-GP) → relax to $C_D\ge C_{d_0}+\dfrac{C_L^2}{\pi e A}$ (eq 14, GP-compatible posynomial ≤ monomial).
Context: a posynomial EQUALITY is not GP-legal; relax to ≥. Tight at optimum **iff**: (a) the relaxed variable (C_D) appears in NO monomial equality, and (b) objective and all other inequality constraints are monotone increasing (or constant) in C_D. Then optimizer drives C_D down to equality for free.
GP-class: eq13 posynomial(=) non-GP → eq14 posynomial(≤) GP | Handling: algebraic-convert (relax = to ≥/≤) | Loc: §II "Posynomial Equality Relaxation", p.TBD
Notes: **THIS is the workhorse for our mass buildup, drag buildup, buoyancy.** Same monotonicity test we must apply to every relaxed equality in Layer-A. Also (E-2 in gpintro, effmdo) restate it for weight buildup W_total ≥ ΣW_i.

### E-6 Objective / "minimize the inverse to maximize"  [block: objective]
LaTeX: to MAXIMIZE a quantity x (velocity, efficiency, **payload**), minimize the monomial $x^{-1}$ (i.e. put 1/x in the posynomial objective).
Context: GP objective must be a posynomial to be MINIMIZED. Maximizing is illegal directly; minimize the reciprocal. Aggregate objectives = positive weighted sum of quantities-to-penalize.
GP-class: monomial objective | Handling: use-as-is | Loc: §III.2.3 Objective, p.TBD
Notes: **Directly answers our task**: "MAXIMIZE PAYLOAD" ⇒ objective = min W_pay^{-1} (or min 1/m_pay). Matches the project design notes rule "use min R⁻¹, not min −R".

LaTeX: $L=W$ (15); $T=D$ (16); $L=\tfrac12\rho V^2 C_L S$ (17); $D\ge\tfrac12\rho V^2 C_D S$ (18). Weight buildup: $W_{zfw}\ge W_{fixed}+W_{pay}+W_{wing}+W_{engine}+\dots$ (19); $W\ge W_{zfw}(1+\theta_{fuel})$ (20). Efficiency chain: $TV\le P_{fuel}\,\eta_{eng}\,\eta_{prop}$ (21).
Context: the most basic aircraft constraints. (15)–(18) are MONOMIAL (equalities as written; D as ≥ inequality). Weight buildups are posynomial ≤.
GP-class: monomial (15-18) / posynomial (19-21) | Handling: use-as-is | Loc: §V "Steady Level Flight", p.TBD

### E-8 Breguet range (GP-compatible via exp Taylor)  [block: template]
LaTeX: $R=\dfrac{h_{fuel}}{g}\eta_0\dfrac{L}{D}\log(1+\theta_{fuel})$ (22, non-GP log). Rewrite $1+\theta_{fuel}=\exp(z)$ with $z=\dfrac{gRD}{h_{fuel}\eta_0 L}$ (24); then $\theta_{fuel}\ge z+\dfrac{z^2}{2!}+\dfrac{z^3}{3!}+\dots$ (25).
Context: constant L/D and η_0 over segment. The log/exp is non-posynomial; the Taylor expansion of exp HAS posynomial structure ⇒ z + z²/2 + z³/6 (≥) is GP-legal. 3-term >99% accurate for θ<0.95; 4-term for θ<2.0. Split long missions into segments for accuracy.
GP-class: eq22 non-GP → eqs 24 (monomial def of z) + 25 (posynomial ≤) GP | Handling: algebraic-convert + Taylor | Loc: §V Breguet, p.TBD
Notes: **Battery-electric analog** (our case, no fuel burn): Breguet-electric uses E_bat = P·t, range = η·E_bat/(g·W)·(L/D). See Berkeley thesis (electric) & Burton-Hoburg solar for the battery form. For our SPRINT we likely use energy = D·V·t/η directly (a monomial), not Breguet.

### E-9 Propulsive (actuator-disk) efficiency  [block: template]

Context: T/(½ρA_prop V²)=C_T/λ_a². The sqrt form is not GP; rearranges to a clean posynomial ≤ constant.
GP-class: eq36 non-GP → eq37 posynomial GP | Handling: algebraic-convert | Loc: §V Propulsive Efficiency, p.TBD

LaTeX (key relations):

## Data fitting with GP-compatible softmax functions — bibkey: hoburg2016data — file: data-fitting-with-...-softmax-3s7ic0rwbb.mmd
Bib: Hoburg, W., Kirschen, P. & Abbeel, P., "Data Fitting with Geometric-Programming-Compatible
Softmax Functions," Optimization and Engineering, Vol. 17, No. 4, 2016, pp. 897–918.
doi:10.1007/s11081-016-9332-3.  **← THE fitting-method paper (gpfit theory).**

Nomenclature (this source):
- u ∈ ℝⁿ₊₊ raw positive vars ; w = raw positive dependent var ; (x,y)=(log u, log w) log-space
- 𝒟 = {(u_i,w_i)} data set ; a_k,b_k = affine-plane params (b_k=log c_k)
- α = softness/sharpness parameter (scalar for SMA; α_k per-term for ISMA)
- 𝓕ᴷ_MA / 𝓕ᴷ_SMA / 𝓕ᴷ_ISMA = max-affine / softmax-affine / implicit-softmax-affine classes, K terms
- K = number of affine terms ; n_p = number of fit parameters ; g(u,w) = fitted GP constraint

### E-13 Monomial, posynomial, GP standard & convex form  [block: method]
LaTeX: posynomial $\sum_k c_k\prod_i u_i^{a_{ik}}$ (eq2); GP standard form $\min g_0$ s.t. $g_i(\mathbf u)\le1$, $h_i(\mathbf u)=1$ (eq3). Convex form via $\mathbf x=\log\mathbf u$ (4): monomial → $\mathbf a^{\mathsf T}\mathbf x+b=0$ (5); posynomial → $\log\sum_k\exp(\mathbf a_k^{\mathsf T}\mathbf x+b_k)\le0$ (6).
Context: same defs; adds the explicit LOG-SUM-EXP convex form (6) — the object every fit targets. Defines **log-log-convex**: f is log-log-convex iff x↦log f(eˣ) is convex.
GP-class: — | Handling: use-as-is (cite) | Loc: §1.1, p.TBD

### E-14 GP-compatible fitting — 3-step procedure + tightness conditions  [block: method]
LaTeX: (1) log-transform $(\mathbf x_i,y_i)=(\log\mathbf u_i,\log w_i)$; (2) fit convex $f$ with $y_i\approx f(\mathbf x_i)$; (3) RELAX equality $y=f(\mathbf x)$ to inequality $y\ge f(\mathbf x)$ ≡ GP constraint $g(\mathbf u,w)\le1$.
Tightness (equality at optimum) sufficient conditions: (a) w appears in no monomial equality; (b) objective + other inequalities monotone increasing in w; (c) fitted g monotone decreasing in w (always holds here).
Context: the master recipe for turning ANY positive tabular CFD data into a GP constraint. Input data must be (i) strictly positive, (ii) well-approximated by a log-log-convex function.
GP-class: method | Handling: use-as-is (this IS our CFD→GP pipeline) | Loc: §1.3 "Overview of GP-compatible fitting", p.TBD
Notes: **CORE OF OUR CALIBRATION STEP.** Our a_peak, C_D0(Λ), k(Λ), C_Lα(Λ), −Cp,min all get fit this way. Check each dependent var's monotonicity to guarantee tight fits.

### E-15 Max-affine class (𝓕ᴷ_MA)  [block: method]
LaTeX: $f_{MA}(\mathbf x)=\max_{k=1..K}\left[b_k+\mathbf a_k^{\mathsf T}\mathbf x\right]$ (eq8)
Context: max of K affine planes; any closed convex fn = sup of affine fns, so 𝓕^∞_MA approximates any convex fn. n_p=K(n+1). Fit by Magnani–Boyd (2008) least-squares partition, or Kim et al. (2010) max-monomial.
GP-class: → max-monomial (GP-compatible) | Handling: fit→monomial set | Loc: §2, eq8, p.TBD

### E-16 Softmax-affine class (𝓕ᴷ_SMA)  [block: method]
LaTeX: $f_{SMA}(\mathbf x)=\dfrac{1}{\alpha}\log\sum_{k=1}^{K}\exp\!\big(\alpha(b_k+\mathbf a_k^{\mathsf T}\mathbf x)\big)$ (eq9). Limit: $\lim_{\alpha\to+\infty}f_{SMA}=f_{MA}$ (eq10).
Context: adds ONE softness param α; n_p=K(n+1)+1. Convex for any α>0. α→∞ recovers max-affine; α=1 recovers a plain posynomial. So SMA UNIFIES max-monomial + posynomial fitting.
GP-class: → posynomial (GP-compatible) | Handling: fit→posynomial | Loc: §3 "Softmax-affine", p.TBD

### E-17 Implicit softmax-affine class (𝓕ᴷ_ISMA) — most expressive  [block: method]
LaTeX: implicit fn $\tilde f_{ISMA}(\mathbf x,y)=\log\sum_{k=1}^{K}\exp\!\big(\alpha_k(b_k+\mathbf a_k^{\mathsf T}\mathbf x-y)\big)$ (eq11); prediction defined by $\tilde f_{ISMA}(\mathbf x,f_{ISMA}(\mathbf x))=0$ (eq12). Per-term α_k. n_p=K(n+2). Hierarchy: $\mathcal F^K_{MA}\subsetneq\mathcal F^K_{SMA}\subsetneq\mathcal F^K_{ISMA}$.
Context: each affine term gets its OWN softness α_k ⇒ can tune softness locally (sharp kinks AND smooth regions in one fit). Propositions 1–2: unique y-zero exists and f_ISMA is convex. Best ISMA fit ≥ best SMA ≥ best MA for any (data,K). Evaluated by Newton iteration (Algorithm 1).
GP-class: → implicit posynomial (GP-compatible) | Handling: fit→implicit posynomial | Loc: §3 "Implicit softmax-affine", p.TBD
Notes: **Use ISMA (gpfit default) for the trickiest CFD surfaces** (e.g. −Cp,min(C_L) near cavitation onset, or C_D0(Λ) across the Λ sweep where behavior kinks).

### E-18 GP transformations of the three classes  [block: method]
LaTeX:
- MA → K monomial inequalities: $\dfrac{e^{b_k}}{w}\prod_i u_i^{a_{ik}}\le1,\ k=1..K$ (eq18)
- SMA → posynomial on wᵅ: $\dfrac{1}{w^\alpha}\sum_{k=1}^{K}e^{\alpha b_k}\prod_i u_i^{\alpha a_{ik}}\le1$ (eq21); at α=1 → plain posynomial $\dfrac{1}{w}\sum_k e^{b_k}\prod_i u_i^{a_{ik}}\le1$ (eq22)
- ISMA → implicit posynomial: $\sum_{k=1}^{K}\dfrac{e^{\alpha_k b_k}}{w^{\alpha_k}}\prod_i u_i^{\alpha_k a_{ik}}\le1$ (eq25)
Context: the exact GP-constraint shapes that go straight into gpkit. Key subtlety: posynomial-fitting = fitting ŵ=g(u) is only SMA with α=1; searching over wᵅ=g(u) (α free) gives strictly better fits.
GP-class: monomial/posynomial/implicit-posynomial | Handling: use-as-is (paste into model) | Loc: §4.1, p.TBD

### E-19 Least-squares fitting objective + LM  [block: method]
LaTeX: $\min_{\beta}\sum_{i=1}^{m}\big(f(\mathbf x_i;\beta)-y_i\big)^2$ (eq26), β = (a,b,α...) parameters, n_p=𝒪(Kn). Solved by Levenberg–Marquardt (non-convex, use random restarts). Cost per LM iter ~𝒪(mK²n²). Parameterize log α (keeps α>0, better conditioning).
Context: how gpfit actually solves the fit. Quality metric = RMS log error.
GP-class: method (offline, not in GP) | Handling: use-as-is | Loc: §5, p.TBD
Notes: 20 random restarts used in the drag example; take best. Persist fits.

## A Tutorial on Geometric Programming — bibkey: boyd2007tutorial — file: gp_tutorial.mmd
Bib: Boyd, S., Kim, S.-J., Vandenberghe, L. & Hassibi, A., "A Tutorial on Geometric Programming,"
Optimization and Engineering, Vol. 8, No. 1, 2007, pp. 67–127. doi:10.1007/s11081-007-9001-7.
(Page 1 dropped in OCR — bib from prior knowledge; venue confirmed by content & citations.)

Nomenclature (this source):

### E-21 Monomial / posynomial / GP standard form  [block: method]
LaTeX: monomial $c\,x_1^{a_1}\cdots x_n^{a_n}$ (eq1, c>0, aᵢ∈ℝ); posynomial $\sum_{k=1}^{K}c_k x_1^{a_{1k}}\cdots x_n^{a_{nk}}$ (eq2). GP standard form $\min f_0$ s.t. $f_i(x)\le1$, $g_i(x)=1$ (eq3), f posynomial, g monomial.
Context: the most-cited canonical GP reference. Notes monomial (GP sense) ≠ algebra monomial (here c any positive, exponents any real). Objective must be posynomial and MINIMIZED; equalities only monomial=1; inequalities only posynomial≤1.
GP-class: — | Handling: use-as-is (cite) | Loc: §2, p.TBD
Notes: Primary citation for definitions in the report.

### E-22 Convexity property of posynomials (weighted geometric mean)  [block: method]
LaTeX: monomial log-transforms to affine (linear equation in yᵢ=log xᵢ); posynomial log-transforms to convex log-sum-exp. Geometric-mean inequality: $f(x^\theta\tilde x^{1-\theta})\le f(x)^\theta f(\tilde x)^{1-\theta}$.
Context: the mathematical fact that makes GP convex and globally solvable.
GP-class: — | Handling: use-as-is (cite) | Loc: §2.3, p.TBD

### E-23 Generalized posynomial / max-of-posynomials (the max trick)  [block: method]
LaTeX: constraint $\max\{f_1,f_2,f_3\}\le1$ (f_i posynomial), not itself posynomial, handled by new var t + upper bounds $f_1\le t, f_2\le t, f_3\le t$ (and objective/other constraints via t). Positive fractional powers of posynomials handled by aux vars similarly. Generalized posynomials closed under +,×,positive powers, max, /monomial, composition.
Context: how to legally include a MAX of posynomials, and fractional powers, without leaving GP (technically GGP). Also handles min via upper-bound variable.
GP-class: generalized posynomial (GGP, GP-solvable) | Handling: algebraic-convert (aux var) | Loc: §5, p.TBD
Notes: **Useful if we need max(air-drag, water-drag) or piecewise medium constraints, or if a max-monomial (kinked) fit is chosen for C_D0(Λ).**

### E-24 GGP definition; exp/1/(1−q) compositions  [block: method]
LaTeX: GGP: $\min f_0$ s.t. $f_i\le1$, $g_i=1$ with f_i generalized posynomials, g_i monomials (eq22). Composition with 1/(1−q): replace by t with $q(x)+1/t\le1$. Exp: $\exp f\approx(1+f/a)^a$ (a large) is a generalized posynomial for small f.
Context: extends the modelable function set; the exp approximation is an alternative to the Breguet Taylor route.
GP-class: GGP | Handling: algebraic-convert | Loc: §5, p.TBD

### E-25 When can data be approximated by a monomial / posynomial?  [block: method]
LaTeX: form $F(y)=\log f(e^y)$. Then: f ≈ monomial ⇔ F ≈ affine; f ≈ generalized posynomial ⇔ F ≈ convex. Convex F ≈ piecewise-linear max of affine → $f(x)\approx\max_i e^{b_{0i}}x_1^{b_{1i}}\cdots x_n^{b_{ni}}$ (max-monomial).
Context: the theoretical basis for ALL GP data fitting — check log-log convexity first (same as softmax paper's precondition).
GP-class: method | Handling: use-as-is (screening test) | Loc: §8 Theory, p.TBD
Notes: **Run this screen on every CFD table before fitting**: log-log-plot each coefficient; if convex → posynomial fit OK; if affine → single monomial suffices (e.g. a_peak).

### E-26 Local monomial approximation (Taylor in log space)  [block: method]
LaTeX: near x, $f(w)\approx f(x)\prod_{i=1}^{n}\big(w_i/x_i\big)^{a_i}$ (eq40) with $a_i=\dfrac{x_i}{f(x)}\dfrac{\partial f}{\partial x_i}$.

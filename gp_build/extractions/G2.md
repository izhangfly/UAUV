# G2 — Wing aerodynamics & skew/sweep dependence (extraction)

Theme: lift, finite-wing lift-curve slope C_Lα, induced drag, aspect ratio, section drag,
Oswald e, and how SWEEP/SKEW angle Λ changes C_Lα / C_D0 / C_l(roll). The Λ-dependence of
these coefficients is to be CFD-FITTED per source; analytic forms below give the functional
skeleton (cosΛ / cos²Λ, AR-collapse) the CFD will calibrate.

> **GLOBAL SKEW/SWEEP CONVENTION QUARANTINE.** Our CAD numbering (the project design notes §0): `WingXX` =
> angle between span and body, so **90 = deployed (perpendicular, air), 0 = stowed (flat along
> body, water)**, and **Λ_pipeline = 90 − XX**. Aerodynamic **sweep Λ** is conventionally
> measured from the deployed/unswept position: **Λ_sweep = 0 = deployed (perp to flow),
> Λ_sweep = 90° = flat along fuselage (fully "swept"/stowed)**. So Λ_sweep = Λ_pipeline here.
> The two `drones` papers below use **sweep = 0 for the deployed max-span (loiter) config**,
> which MATCHES the aero convention and our pipeline Λ — their 0→60°(→90°) sweep sweep data
> maps directly onto our deployed→stowed skew. **Beware every source's local symbol for Λ, Γ,
> δ, e, α — collisions flagged per source.**

## Fluid-Dynamic Lift (2nd ed.) — bibkey: hoerner1985fluid — file: Fluid-dynamic_lift__Hoerner__1985_text.mmd
Bib: Hoerner, S.F. & Borst, H.V., *Fluid-Dynamic Lift*, 2nd ed., Hoerner Fluid Dynamics, 1985.
Reference textbook for the analytic wing skeleton. Figure-heavy OCR (9587 lines); several
equation lines land on `[MISSING_PAGE_FAIL:n]` and are noted.

Nomenclature (this source — CLASH-HEAVY):
- `A` = aspect ratio (Hoerner writes `A`, not `AR`); `A = b²/S` (also `A = b/c̄` for const chord). `A_p`, `A_i` = panel / effective aspect ratio.
- `α` = angle of attack; `α_i` = induced angle; `α_g` = geometric AoA. **"lift angle" = dα/dC_L = 1/slope** (Hoerner works with the RECIPROCAL of the lift-curve slope).
- `Γ` (Gamma) = **DIHEDRAL angle** in Hoerner — NOT sweep. His cos²Γ law is for dihedral/V-tail. (Do not confuse with our skew Λ.)
- `Λ` (Lambda) = **sweep angle** in Hoerner's swept-wing / aileron sections (e.g. cos²Λ aileron law). ⚠ Also OCR sometimes mangles the aspect ratio `A` into `Λ` (see E-8) — read by context.
- `τ` (tau) = a lift-slope constant ≈ π (appears in the cavity/finite-AR slope formulas E-8).
- `ξ` / `c̄` = mean chord; `b` = span; `S` = area; `q` = ½ρV².

Context: (Eq.19) = the exact regime of our STOWED wing (A→0.1) — reproduces the project design notes

wetted (air/underwater deployed), planing, and supercavitating slopes over finite A — useful

GP-class: a_3D(A) forms are monomial-in-ratio (posynomial-compatible after fixing A per case). | Handling: use-as-is for regime bracketing; fit→posynomial for the CFD-measured C_Lα(Λ). | Loc: "Small aspect ratios" Eq.19 (L2199–2201); cavity slopes Eqs 53–55 (L2681–2687).

Bib **(CONFIRMED from source PDF, was OCR-empty):** Chen, Qian (陈钱); Bai, Peng (白鹏); Li, Feng

**WHY THIS ONE MATTERS MOST OF THE FOUR:** it directly compares two variable-sweep MECHANISMS:
- **旋转变后掠 "rotating variable-sweep"** = whole wing pivots about a body-mounted axis → wing

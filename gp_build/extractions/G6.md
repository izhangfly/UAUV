## Structural Optimization Applied to Submarine Pressure Hulls — bibkey: structhull — file: v1_covered_6118867c-821d-4d75-9e57-3457ab40ce56.mmd
**Bib (VERIFIED):** Shinoka, Thiago Kenji Leao & Netto, Theodoro Antoun. 2024/2025. "Structural
Optimization Applied to Submarine Pressure Hulls." Dept. of Ocean Engineering, COPPE —
Universidade Federal do Rio de Janeiro. Research Square preprint, posted 18 Nov 2024,
DOI 10.21203/rs.3.rs-5363785/v1. **Version of record:** *Journal of Ocean Engineering and Marine
Energy*, 4 Jan 2025, DOI 10.1007/s40722-024-00376-4. → bibkey **structhull CONFIRMED** (title matches exactly).

**What it is for G6:** analytical ring-stiffened cylindrical pressure-hull model with THREE limit
states — (1) overall/general collapse [Bryant 1954], (2) interframe collapse [Kendrick von Mises],
(3) stiffener tripping [MacKay 2007]. Objective = minimize hull weight (steel volume) s.t. the three
limit states with partial safety factors. This is exactly the G6 skeleton, but written for a stiffened
hull, so the "hoop stress" and "buckling" appear in richer stiffened forms (unstiffened membrane forms
recovered as limits — see notes).

### Nomenclature (this source) — QUARANTINE (heavy clashes with the rest of the GP model)
- **a = shell mean RADIUS** (NOT acceleration; this source's "R"). Table 1: a=4.90 m (Design A), 3.02 m (B). **Clash: elsewhere R is radius, here a.**
- **h = SHELL PLATING THICKNESS** (NOT depth!). Bounds 5–60 mm. **CRITICAL CLASH: the G6 theme uses h for depth; here h = wall thickness (the "t" of t≥pR/σ). Map: structhull h ⇒ GP t; GP depth ⇒ use symbol H or h_d.**
- **L = frame spacing** (unsupported bay length between ring stiffeners); **L_f** same (Eq 1); **L_B = length between rigid bulkheads** (17.86 m A / 9.00 m B).
- **P = external (hydrostatic) pressure**; P_N overall elastic buckling p; P_cr,0 overall elastic buckling (min form); P_m1 Kendrick von-Mises interframe buckling p; P_c5 plate circumferential-yield p; P_fy stiffener-flange-yield p; P_cl interframe collapse p.
- **n = circumferential buckling lobe number** (integer ≥2); **m = axial half-wave number**; **λ = πa/L** slenderness (implied).
- **σ_y = yield stress = 575.7 MPa (HY-80 steel)**; **σ_yf** flange yield; **σ_yp** plate yield; **σ_fy** total stress in stiffener; **σ_t** tripping stress.
- **E = 206.8 GPa**, **μ = Poisson ratio = 0.3**. ρ_steel ≈ 7850 kg/m³ (implied; densities of plate & stiffener taken equal).
- Stiffener section: **h_f** flange thickness, **f** flange width, **h_w** web thickness, **d** web depth/width; **A_f** frame cross-section area; **a_f** radius to frame centroid; **e_f** dist. to flange; **I_c** 2nd moment of combined ring+effective plate; **I_z** 2nd moment for tripping; **a_gc** radius to centroid of combined section; **a_1** a length param in tripping.
- **C_0 = out-of-circularity (OOC) imperfection amplitude = 0.4%·r**; γ,β,G,N,A,α = Bryant/Kendrick intermediate coefficients.
- **SF = partial safety factor** (base case: 2.0 overall, 1.5 interframe); **P_co/P_d = collapse/design pressure ratio** (design target 1.73–2.00 for ≥200 m depth).

### E-1 Overall (general-instability) elastic buckling pressure — Bryant (1954) [block: structure/buckling]
LaTeX:
$$P_N=\frac{\left(\frac{E\,h}{a}\right)\lambda^{4}}{\left(n^{2}-1+\frac{\lambda^{2}}{2}\right)\left(n^{2}+\lambda^{2}\right)^{2}}+\frac{(n^{2}-1)\,E\,I_c}{a\,a_{gc}^{2}\,L_f}\qquad(\lambda=\pi a/L)$$
Context: buckling pressure of a simply-supported ring-stiffened cylinder = (von-Mises shell term) + (Bresse ring term). n minimized over integer lobes. First term = shell contribution; second = ring+plating contribution.
**GP-class: SIGNOMIAL** (denominator `(n²−1+λ²/2)(n²+λ²)²` is a posynomial in the *denominator* → not GP; n is a discrete inner-minimization). **Handling: fit→monomial** (recommended) or SP. **Loc:** §2.2.1 Eq (1), p.5.
Notes: DO NOT DROP — this is the general-instability collapse pressure the buoyancy-vs-strength trade turns on. Recommended GP path: for the FIXED small-diameter HAUV hull (thin unstiffened or lightly stiffened tube), fit a monomial `p_cr ≈ C·E·(t/a)^α·(a/L)^β` over the (t/a, L/a) box from a handful of evaluations (or adopt Windenburg–Trilling closed form, see E-8). Keep n-minimization OUT of the GP (evaluate offline, bake C,α,β).

### E-2 Total (axisymmetric + bending) stress in ring stiffener [block: structure/stress]
LaTeX:
$$\sigma_{fy}=\frac{P}{P_{fy}}\,\sigma_{yf}+\frac{(n^{2}-1)\,E\,e_f}{a^{2}}\left(\frac{P}{P_{cr,0}-P}\right)C_0\cos(n\theta)\sin\!\left(\frac{m\pi x}{L_B}\right)$$
Context: combined membrane + imperfection-amplified bending stress in the frame; the `P/(P_cr,0−P)` factor is the classic imperfection amplification (blows up as P→P_cr,0). C_0 = OOC amplitude.
**GP-class: SIGNOMIAL** (the `P/(P_cr,0−P)` denominator difference + trig terms). **Handling: SP or drop for v1** (imperfection-sensitivity refinement; the tube here is near-perfect/short). **Loc:** §2.2.1 Eq (2), p.5.
Notes: keep conceptually (amplification factor motivates the SF), but not a GP constraint for a first model.

### E-3 External pressure causing yield in stiffener flange [block: structure/stress]
LaTeX:
$$P_{fy}=\frac{h\,\sigma_{yf}\,a_f}{a^{2}\left(1-\frac{\mu}{2}-\gamma\right)}$$
Context: pressure at which the ring flange first yields; γ from E-11.
**GP-class: near-MONOMIAL** in {h, σ_yf, a_f} but denominator `(1−μ/2−γ)` is a signomial constant-ish factor (γ depends on section). **Handling: algebraic-convert / treat (1−μ/2−γ) as a fitted constant → monomial.** **Loc:** §2.2.1 Eq (3), p.5.

### E-4 Overall elastic buckling pressure (governing, min form) [block: structure/buckling]
LaTeX:
$$P_{cr,0}=\min\!\left[\,P_N,\ \frac{N}{D}\cdot\frac{E\,h}{a\,(1-\mu^{2})}\right]$$
Context: governing overall elastic buckling pressure = min of Bryant P_N (E-1) and the classical thin-cylinder term `Eh/(a(1−μ²))` scaled by mode factor N/D. The second term is the pure-shell instability.
**GP-class:** `min[...]` → in GP a `min` on the *capacity* side becomes TWO separate `≥` constraints (both must exceed demand), which is GP-legal. The `Eh/(a(1−μ²))` piece is MONOMIAL in h. P_N piece is E-1 (fit). **Handling: use-as-is as two constraints** (p_demand·SF ≤ P_N and ≤ shell term). **Loc:** §2.2.1 Eq (4), p.5.
Notes: This is the KEEP buckling constraint. GP form: `SF_ov · p_d ≤ P_cr,0` split into two monomial/fitted inequalities.

### E-5 Yield limit state with safety factor [block: structure/safety]
LaTeX: $$SF\cdot\sigma_{fy} < \sigma_{y}$$
Context: total stress × partial SF must stay below material yield. Base-case SF: 2.0 (overall), 1.5 (interframe).
**GP-class: MONOMIAL inequality (posynomial ≤ const).** **Handling: use-as-is** (`SF·σ ≤ σ_y`). **Loc:** §2.2.1 Eq (5), p.5.
Notes: GP-GOLD template for every strength gauge constraint. Also drives the classic gauge `t ≥ SF·p_d·a/σ_y` (see E-0 derived).

### E-6 Interframe (inter-stiffener) collapse pressure — plate buckling between rings [block: structure/buckling]
LaTeX:
$$\frac{P_{cl}}{P_{c5}}=\frac{P_{m1}}{P_{c5}}\ \text{ for }\frac{P_{m1}}{P_{c5}}<1.0;\qquad \frac{P_{cl}}{P_{c5}}\cong 1-\frac{P_{m1}}{2P_{c5}}\ \text{ for }\frac{P_{m1}}{P_{c5}}\ge 1.0$$
Context: empirical (experiment-fit) interframe collapse pressure P_cl; interpolates between von-Mises elastic buckling P_m1 (E-7) and plate circumferential yield P_c5 (E-8). Shell plating assumed simply supported by rings.
**GP-class: SIGNOMIAL** (piecewise + the `1 − P_m1/2P_c5` difference). **Handling: SP, or for v1 approximate P_cl ≈ min(P_m1, P_c5)** (conservative, GP-legal as two constraints). **Loc:** §2.2.2 Eq (6), p.6 [recovered from MISSING_PAGE_FAIL:6 via PDF].
Notes: KEEP — interframe collapse is the second independent buckling mode; small-bay hulls are often interframe-governed. The `min(P_m1,P_c5)` surrogate is the clean GP handling.

### E-7 Kendrick's von-Mises interframe elastic buckling pressure P_m1 [block: structure/buckling]
LaTeX:
$$P_{m1}=\frac{E\,h}{a}\left[n^{2}-1+\tfrac{1}{2}\!\left(\tfrac{\pi a}{L}\right)^{2}\right]^{-1}\left\{\left[n^{2}\!\left(\tfrac{L}{\pi a}\right)^{2}+1\right]^{-2}+\frac{h^{2}}{12a^{2}(1-\mu^{2})}\left[n^{2}-1+\left(\tfrac{\pi a}{L}\right)^{2}\right]^{2}\right\}$$
Context: elastic buckling pressure of the plate bay between rings (classic von Mises, Kendrick form). Bending stiffness term `∝ h²/(12a²(1−μ²))` = shell flexural rigidity. Minimized over n.
**GP-class: SIGNOMIAL** (bracketed inverse-posynomial factors). **Handling: fit→monomial** `P_m1 ≈ C'·E·(h/a)^p·(a/L)^q` (this is the target external-pressure-buckling monomial the theme asks for). **Loc:** §2.2.2 Eq (7), p.6.
Notes: THE key external-pressure buckling law to fit. For a thin unstiffened tube (HAUV likely unstiffened), the whole hull IS one interframe bay (L→L_B), so E-7 ≈ the hull buckling pressure directly.

### E-8 Plate circumferential-yield pressure P_c5 (→ hoop-stress gauge) [block: structure/stress]
LaTeX: $$P_{c5}=\frac{h\,\sigma_{yp}}{a\,(1+\gamma\,G)}$$
Context: external pressure causing yield in the circumferential (hoop) direction at the mid-surface of the plate midway between stiffeners. γ,G from E-9…E-14.
**GP-class:** MONOMIAL `hσ_yp/a` × signomial correction `1/(1+γG)`. **Handling: algebraic-convert** — in the **unstiffened / no-bending limit γG→0**, this collapses to **`P_c5 = h·σ_yp/a`, i.e. the classic membrane HOOP-STRESS gauge `σ_hoop = P·a/h`**. **Loc:** §2.2.2 Eq (8), p.6.
Notes: **This is the source's hoop-stress relation.** GP-GOLD gauge constraint (see E-0). Treat (1+γG) as a fitted knockdown constant ≈1 for a thin tube.

### E-9…E-14 Kendrick intermediate coefficients [block: structure/aux]
LaTeX:
$$G=\frac{-2\left[\sinh\frac{\alpha L}{2}\cos\frac{\alpha L}{2}+\cosh\frac{\alpha L}{2}\sin\frac{\alpha L}{2}\right]}{\sinh\alpha L+\sin\alpha L}\ (9);\quad \alpha=\sqrt[4]{\frac{3(1-\mu^{2})}{a^{2}h^{2}}}\ (10)$$
$$\gamma=\frac{A\left(1-\frac{\mu}{2}\right)}{(A+h\,h_w)(1+\beta)}\ (11);\quad \beta=\frac{2hN}{\alpha(A+h\,h_w)}\ (12);\quad N=\frac{\cosh\alpha L-\cos\alpha L}{\sinh\alpha L+\sin\alpha L}\ (13);\quad A=\frac{a^{2}}{a_f^{2}}A_f\ (14)$$
Context: dimensionless factors coupling ring stiffness to plate (α = shell decay length param, Eq 10 is a monomial in a,h). Feed γ,G into E-8 and γ into E-3.
**GP-class: mostly SIGNOMIAL** (hyperbolic/trig functions of αL). Eq (10) α and Eq (14) A are MONOMIAL. **Handling: evaluate offline / fit** — bake γ,G as fitted constants for the design box; only α (E-10) and A (E-14) are directly GP-usable. **Loc:** §2.2.2 Eqs (9)–(14), p.6.

### E-15/E-16/E-17 Stiffener tripping checks — MacKay (2007) [block: structure/stiffener]
LaTeX:
$$f_1=\frac{d}{h_w}\sqrt{\frac{\sigma_{yf}}{E}}<1.1\ (15)\quad\text{(web buckling)};\qquad f_2=\frac{d}{2h_f}\sqrt{\frac{\sigma_{yf}}{E}}<0.5\ (16)\quad\text{(flange buckling)};\qquad f_3=\frac{\sigma_{yf}}{\sigma_t}<1.1\ (17)\quad\text{(sideways tripping)}$$
Context: three slenderness/strength checks preventing local stiffener failure. σ_t from E-18.
**GP-class: MONOMIAL inequalities** (E-15, E-16 are pure monomials in d,h_w,h_f; E-17 is monomial once σ_t known). **Handling: use-as-is** (only if the HAUV hull is ring-stiffened; likely DROP for an unstiffened thin tube). **Loc:** §2.2.3 Eqs (15)–(17), p.6.

### E-18/E-19 Tripping stress and flange centroid [block: structure/stiffener]
LaTeX:
$$\sigma_t=\frac{E\,I_z}{A_f\,x_f\,a_1}\ (18);\qquad x_f=\frac{d^{2}h_w+f\,h_f(2d+h_f)}{2\,(d\,h_w+f\,h_f)}\ (19)$$
Context: energy-based tripping stress (zero rotational constraint at stiffener–shell intersection); x_f = neutral-axis location of the stiffener section.
**GP-class:** E-18 MONOMIAL; **E-19 SIGNOMIAL** (posynomial/posynomial ratio). **Handling: E-18 use-as-is; E-19 fit or evaluate offline.** **Loc:** §2.2.3 Eqs (18)–(19), p.6.

### E-OBJ Objective + design pressure + buoyancy factor [block: objective]
Text (no single boxed eq): "The objective function defined is to **minimize the weight of the pressure
hull** while subject to the restrictions defined limit states" (§2.3.2, p.6). Steel VOLUME minimized
(plate ρ = stiffener ρ assumed equal). Loads (§3.2, p.7): outer uniform hydrostatic pressure P + **axial
end-cap load = P·π·a²** on the edges. Design/collapse ratio **P_co/P_d = 1.73–2.00** for ≥200 m depth (§4, p.16).
- **Weight / steel-volume objective (monomial+posynomial):** `W = ρ_s·g·[ 2π a L_B h  +  N_stiff·A_f·2π a ]` → **posynomial in (a, L_B, h, A_f, N_stiff)**. GP: minimize W (or minimize buoyancy factor BF).
- **Buoyancy factor** BF = W_structure / B_displaced (from intro; Imran/Yang minimize BF) → **posynomial/monomial ratio**; minimize BF or constrain BF ≤ target. Payload-max objective = maximize (B − W_structure − W_fixed).
- **Design pressure** `p_d = ρ_w g H` (depth H) — MONOMIAL (implied by "Water Depth 400/500 m"; not boxed).
**GP-class: OBJECTIVE posynomial (min W or min BF = GP-legal; matches the project design notes "min R⁻¹" spirit).** **Handling: use-as-is.** **Loc:** §2.3.2 + §3.2 + Table 1, p.5–7.
Notes: end-cap axial stress `σ_ax = Pπa²/(2πa h) = P a/(2h)` = ½ hoop — standard, add if axial gauge needed.

### E-0 (DERIVED, standard) Hoop-stress gauge + design pressure — for the GP core
LaTeX (thin-shell membrane, external pressure):
$$\sigma_{\text{hoop}}=\frac{p_d\,a}{h}\ \Rightarrow\ \boxed{\,h \ge \frac{SF\cdot p_d\,a}{\sigma_y}\,},\qquad p_d=\rho_w\,g\,H$$
Context: NOT boxed in structhull, but it is the **unstiffened γG→0 limit of E-8** (P_c5=hσ_yp/a) and the membrane precursor to all the above. This is the G6 **GP-GOLD gauge constraint**.
**GP-class: MONOMIAL (both).** **Handling: use-as-is** — the strength-side gauge. Pair with a buckling constraint (E-4/E-7 fit or Windenburg–Trilling) as the stability-side gauge; the hull thickness is set by whichever governs (`h ≥ max(strength, buckling)` → two GP inequalities).
Notes: For a slender HAUV tube at shallow depth, buckling almost always governs over hoop yield → the fitted buckling monomial (E-7) is the binding constraint; keep both.

**RECOMMENDED external-pressure buckling monomial (Windenburg–Trilling, standard closed form — NOT in this
paper, cited for the fit target the theme requested):**
$$p_{cr}\approx\frac{2.42\,E\,(h/2a)^{2.5}}{(1-\mu^{2})^{0.75}\,\left[\frac{L}{2a}-0.45\,(h/2a)^{0.5}\right]}$$
→ SIGNOMIAL (denominator difference). GP path: **SP**, or **monomial fit** `p_cr = C·E·(h/a)^{2.5}(a/L)` over the design box (drop the small −0.45√(h/2a) term for a monomial lower bound). This is the concrete realization of the theme's `p_cr ≈ C·E·(t/R)^2.5/(L/R − a·(t/R)^0.5)`.

### E-R1 Buoyancy balance / neutral-buoyancy principle [block: objective/buoyancy]
LaTeX: $$B=\nabla\,\rho\,g\ ;\qquad \text{equilibrium: mass balanced exactly by buoyancy } (W=B)$$
Context: Archimedes — the whole "buoyancy-capped" premise. Weight must equal displaced-water buoyancy for neutral trim; structural weight competes directly with payload. Distinguishes **hydrostatic displacement** (excl. free-flood water, naval-architect definition) vs **form displacement** (incl. free-flood water, hydrodynamicist definition).
**GP-class: MONOMIAL (B=ρg∇).** **Handling: use-as-is** — the buoyancy cap constraint `W_total ≤ ρ_w g ∇_hull`. **Loc:** §2.1 (Buoyancy & stability), p.~lines 339, 153.
Notes: This is the buoyancy ceiling the payload-max objective sits under (matches the project design notes §3 buoyancy budget).

## Summary of G6 GP building blocks (priority order)
1. **Hoop-stress gauge (E-0 / E-8 limit):** `h ≥ SF·p_d·a/σ_y`, `p_d = ρ_w g H` — MONOMIAL, use-as-is. GP-GOLD.
2. **External-pressure buckling (E-4/E-7, or Windenburg–Trilling):** collapse pressure — SIGNOMIAL denominators → **fit to monomial** `p_cr = C·E·(h/a)^α(a/L)^β`, enforce `p_cr ≥ SF·p_d`. KEEP (do not drop).
3. **Interframe collapse (E-6/E-7):** second buckling mode — surrogate `P_cl ≈ min(P_m1, P_c5)` (two GP constraints) or SP.
4. **Min-weight / buoyancy-factor objective (E-OBJ):** `W = ρ_s g(2πaL_B h + N·A_f·2πa)` posynomial; minimize W or BF, cap by buoyancy (E-R1). GP-legal.
5. **Yield SF constraint (E-5):** `SF·σ ≤ σ_y` — MONOMIAL, use-as-is.
- Stiffener tripping (E-15…E-19): monomial checks, **DROP unless the HAUV hull is ring-stiffened** (likely unstiffened thin tube at these scales).
- Materials (structhull, HY-80): E = 206.8 GPa, μ = 0.3, σ_y = 575.7 MPa, ρ_s ≈ 7850 kg/m³. (For a small HAUV, substitute Al 6061/7075 or CFRP props — swap E, σ_y, ρ; equations unchanged.)
